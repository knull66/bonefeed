FAQ

What's the point if it can't make money?

Missing a paid P2P, an Earn unlock, or a dump while you're in another window also costs money. Bonefeed is the alarm clock — not the exchange. It alerts. You decide.

What is VIP Signals?

A tighter market desk (±2.5% pump/dump, earlier fee pings, shorter cooldown). It includes Pro. It still never places trades — you keep the keys.

Does Bonefeed trade for me?

No. Watch-only by design. It cannot place orders, withdraw, or sign. You keep control on Binance / your wallet.

Is my Binance key safe?

Stored encrypted locally (AES-GCM + Keychain wrap). Create a read-only key with no withdraw permissions.

Can I watch MetaMask?

Yes — paste your ETH account address (0x…). Bonefeed never connects to the extension or asks for a seed.

Does P2P trading work in Bonefeed?

No trading — watch-only. Pro tracks your Binance P2P orders and keeps a notch timer while one is open. It never releases coins or chats for you.

macOS requirements?

macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Looks best on a MacBook with a notch; works as a top-center island elsewhere.
